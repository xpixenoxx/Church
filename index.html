What we believe

About

  • What we believe
  • It's about Jesus
  • Who we are
  • Our vision and commitments

God made us to know Him and to live in a way that brings Him glory.

The Bible is God's written Word. It is true, trustworthy and the guide we follow in faith and in daily life.

In Jesus Christ, God came to live among us. Jesus lived without sin, died on the cross in our place and rose again, so that we can be forgiven and brought back to God.

We begin a relationship with God when we turn away from sin and put our trust in Christ. It is a gift of grace, not something we earn.

The Holy Spirit lives in every believer, giving us strength to follow Jesus and shaping us in love, joy, patience and goodness.

The church is God's family. We gather to worship Him, and we go out to serve our neighbours the way Jesus served people.

Jesus will return one day to make all things new.

Read our full statement of faith, or write to us if you have questions.

Everything we do begins and ends with Jesus. He is the way, the truth and the life, and He invites every person to know Him.

If you would like to talk about faith, or take your first step, our pastors would be glad to meet you. Get in touch.

Morph Ministries is a growing family of believers from many languages, communities and walks of life across India, united by faith in Jesus Christ.
